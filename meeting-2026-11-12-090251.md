# Summary

## Overview
Speaker 1 and Speaker 2 reviewed proposed DevOps and architecture improvements, including repository consolidation, QA environments, documentation, observability, and access management. They agreed to prepare presentation-level material and audience-specific Miro diagrams to support review and discussion with John.

## Key points
- Merging a change triggers a Terraform apply and provides results.
- Proposed monorepos would consolidate DevOps configuration—Terraform, GitOps, Ansible, and scripts—and platform/product repositories. The LMS/Hub discussion involves sixteen repositories and needs application developer input.
- QA needs a flexible lab capable of supporting multiple environments, targeted testing, and rapid proof-of-concept work. A release manifest would identify what QA is testing.
- Existing Confluence and product documentation contains useful information, but many pages are outdated. Documentation priorities are DevOps, cloud architecture, and product knowledge.
- Observability tools are deployed, but Speaker 2 questioned whether they are being used effectively, particularly for alerting.
- Centralizing access through Microsoft Entra ID requires clearer GitHub and Google Cloud roles, groups, permissions, and mappings. Group membership would provide default access, with PAM supporting elevated access.
- GitOps work needs centralization, and current SDLC activities need simplification.
- Speaker 2 is reviewing repositories, checking AI-generated findings against Google Cloud, and creating architecture diagrams. Their findings indicate that workflows automate development, while staging and production require manual work after PR approvals.

## Decisions
- Review monorepo pros and cons with the team and application developers, covering LMS/Hub and the additional product grouping identified by Speaker 1.
- Enable QA to provision environments through Terraform, also exercising the Terraform configuration.
- Include enabling alerting as an explicit improvement.
- Plan, review, and change permissions and group structures to support Entra ID integration and meaningful PAM entitlements.
- Add GitOps centralization and SDLC simplification to the proposal.
- Keep initial materials at presentation level, with current state, future state, problems, and proposed approaches. Detailed Confluence work should follow John’s review and approval.
- Prepare an HTML presentation and consistent Miro views for the CTO, QA, DevOps, and application developers.
- Use a private Miro scratchpad space for draft boards; either separate boards or one combined board is acceptable.

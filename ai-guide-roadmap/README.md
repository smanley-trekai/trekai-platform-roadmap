# Working with AI agents in software development and DevOps

**New here?** Open the [learning roadmap](visuals/ai-agents-learning-roadmap.html) in your browser. It starts with a simple question: if you give an agent a ticket, how will you know it did the job?

## What are you trying to do?

- **Explain it to the team.** Open the [presentation](visuals/ai-agents-team-presentation.html). Walk through the catalog API example together. When staging fails, let the team pick what to check next. Press **→** to reveal or advance, **←** to go back, **T** for topics, **N** for notes, and **F** for fullscreen.
- **Try it on a ticket.** Open the [engineering field guide](visuals/professional-it-agent-guide.html). Pick [building a new service](visuals/professional-it-agent-guide.html#new) or [fixing a staging failure](visuals/professional-it-agent-guide.html#ongoing). Each step shows what to ask, what the agent should bring back, and what you check.
- **Find words for your first prompt.** Pick a [prompt example](visuals/professional-it-agent-guide.html#prompt-style) and replace the sample task with yours.
- **Finish the ticket.** Use the [handoff example](visuals/professional-it-agent-guide.html#project-context) to save the fix and its test where the next engineer can find them.
